# Backlink plan for homacalculator.com

Prepared for the planned public domain. No outreach or external posting has been sent. No existing backlink profile is assumed.

## Assets already built

- Permanent calculator URLs with readable formulas and worked examples.
- A HOMA1/HOMA2 comparison that avoids mislabelled calculator results.
- 33 reference-linked FAQ answers with direct answer links.
- A reference library with citation-copy and clean-link buttons.
- Local calculation with no lab values in share URLs.

These are reasons a publisher might choose to cite the resource; they are not acquired backlinks.

## Prioritized acquisition work

| Priority | Prospect type | Relevant asset | Qualification before contact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Existing professional or academic collaborators | HOMA method page | Resource page accepts independent educational references; verify clinical review first |
| 1 | Diabetes educators and laboratory education publishers | Unit converters | Their readers need explicit units/factors; verify the method matches their assay conventions |
| 2 | Evidence-focused PCOS or metabolic-health publishers | FAQ and interpretation guide | Their article discusses fasting indices without claiming a standalone diagnosis |
| 2 | Research teaching resources | QUICKI and TyG method pages | Curriculum includes the same equation and formula convention |
| 3 | Relevant calculator directories | Calculator directory | Genuine editorial review, useful categories and no link-exchange requirement |

Create a small qualified prospect list, checking current contact information and submission policies on each publisher's own site. No named organization is represented as having agreed to list or endorse EndoCalc.

## Suggested outreach draft

Subject: A transparent [HOMA1 / unit conversion] resource for your readers

Hello [name],

Your page on [specific topic and URL] discusses [specific reader need]. We have published a free EndoCalc tool at [exact calculator URL] that displays its equation, units, worked example and source references. It explicitly distinguishes HOMA1 from HOMA2 and does not interpret a score as a diagnosis.

If it fits your editorial criteria, would you consider it as a supplementary educational reference? [Only add a statement about independent medical review after that review has actually occurred.]

Thank you,
[real name and affiliation]

## Tracking

Record prospect URL, relevance, date checked, permitted submission channel, owner, contact status, resulting referring page, target calculator and follow-up date. Measure actual referring domains and relevant visits after launch, not the number of emails sent.

Do not buy ranking links, promise reciprocal placements, mass-post forum comments or insert links into unrelated pages. If you later pay for an advertisement, disclose the relationship and use the appropriate sponsored attribute. [Google's link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
